## Concepts

| id | kind | tier | type | statement | introducing span |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `rootish-array-stack` | result | Advanced | — | A RootishArrayStack stores the list in r blocks numbered 0, …, r − 1, block b holding b + 1 elements, so list index i lies in block b = i2b(i) at j = i − b(b+1)/2; get(i) and set(i, x) take O(1) time, add(i, x) and remove(i) O(1 + n − i), any m of them spend O(m) time in grow() and shrink(), and n elements take n + O(√n) words. | `rootish`, "stores its elements in a list of r arrays called blocks" |
| `rootish-index-math` | skill | Beyond | — | ⌊√x⌋ is not a word-RAM operation but can be computed in constant time for every x ≤ n after O(√n) preprocessing: round x down to x′, a multiple of 2^⌊r/2⌋, which costs at most 1 in the root (Lemma: √(x − a) ≥ √x − 1 for 0 ≤ a ≤ √x), look ⌊√x′⌋ up in a table sqrttab, and add 1 at most twice; a table logtab gives ⌊log x⌋ to upgrade small x. | `rootish-sqrt`, "the square root operation can be implemented efficiently" |
| `sqrt-space-lower-bound` | result | Advanced | — | A RootishArrayStack wastes O(√n) space, and no structure that starts empty and adds items one at a time can do better: with r blocks, either r ≥ √n and the r references to them are wasted, or some block holds at least n/r > √n items and was wasting √n space when it was allocated. | `sqrt-lower-bound`, "this space usage is optimal for any data structure that starts out empty and can support the addition of one item at a time" |
| `space-efficient-list` | result | Advanced | — | An SEList with block size b is a doubly-linked list of nodes, each holding a BDeque of capacity b + 1 in which every block but the last keeps between b − 1 and b + 1 elements; locating index i takes O(1 + min{i, n − i}/b) steps, add(i, x) and remove(i) shift through at most b blocks or spread or gather b of them, and the wasted space is O(b + n/b). | `selist`, "reduces this wasted space using a simple idea" |
| `selist-spread-gather` | result | Advanced | cost | Spreading b full blocks into b + 1 (and gathering b blocks of b − 1 into b − 1) costs O(b²), but counting fragile nodes (blocks not holding exactly b elements) shows that from an empty SEList any m ≥ 1 calls to add(i, x) and remove(i) spend O(bm) time in all calls to spread() and gather(). | `selist-amortized`, the lemma's statement "the total time spent during all calls to spread() and gather() is O(bm)" |
